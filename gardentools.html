How it works

One sharpening day a week

No appointments, no waiting in. Get your tools in by the cutoff and they come back sharp the same week.

1

Drop off

Book in on this page by Sunday 3:30pm, then put your tools in the drop-off bin at my house in Newbury by 4pm. Tie a tag on with your name, and take a photo of them before they go in, so you've got a before shot. Then send a quick WhatsApp so I know they're there.

2

Sharpened

Sunday evening and Monday morning, everything from the drop-off bin gets done as one batch. It's not that your secateurs take two days, it's that they wait their turn with everyone else's. If something needs a repair, you get a price by WhatsApp before any work starts.

3

Collect

From Tuesday midday your tools are in the collection bin with the edges guarded. You get a WhatsApp with the total and how to pay. Pay, then collect.

Hand tools only. No mowers, strimmers or anything with an engine. If it's bigger than a spade, send a photo first.

Prices

From £8 a tool

Sharpening is £8 per tool, whatever it is. Simple repairs up to £50, always quoted first.

A tool is one item: a pair of secateurs counts as one, a pair of shears counts as one. Anything not on the list, send a photo and ask.

The small print

Straight answers

There are two bins at my house in Newbury, one for drop-off and one for collection, and they're not the same bin. Tools are at your own risk while they sit in the drop-off bin waiting for me, and again from the moment they go in the collection bin. Tag everything with your name. Don't leave cash in either. The bins are covered by CCTV.

Where the bins are and how they open comes by WhatsApp once you've booked in.

Take a photo of your tools before they go in the drop-off bin. I take one when I pick them up. Between us there's no argument about what arrived. Existing chips, cracks, rust and wobbly handles are on record. Sharpening removes metal. A badly pitted or previously over-ground edge will come back sharp but won't come back new.

If something isn't worth sharpening, I'll tell you rather than take your money.

When your tools are done you get a WhatsApp with the total and your reference. Everything you need to pay is at mrnickpearson.com/pay: bank transfer, which is free and instant, a card button if you'd rather, or exact cash at the door by arrangement. I don't carry change. Tools go in the collection bin once payment has landed.

Tools not collected within four weeks of being ready will be treated as abandoned.

Everything comes back with the edge guarded, taped or sheathed. It's sharper than when it went in. Treat it that way.

I don't take calls from numbers I don't know and I don't ring back. Message me instead: WhatsApp, email or the form, all of them get a reply.

The sharpening day is fixed so the work gets done in one go. Book in by Sunday 3:30pm and have the tools in the drop-off bin by 4pm. Miss that and they simply go into the following week's batch, no drama, just book a bit earlier next time. The days may change, and the page will always show the current ones. Nothing is done outside the batch, however small.
